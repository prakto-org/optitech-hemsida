OptiTech turns your live compliance data into reports and analytics, so you can see where you stand and hand an assessor exactly what they need. Everything below is generated from your controls, evidence, and framework status in real time, from the OptiTech Console.

## Dashboards

Dashboards give you posture at a glance: control coverage, evidence freshness, open findings, and upcoming deadlines, scoped to a program or a domain. Use them for your own day-to-day view and for status updates to stakeholders.

## Framework reports

For each framework you've activated, a framework report shows requirement-by-requirement progress: what's compliant, what's partial, what's missing, and the evidence behind each control. It's the view an auditor walks through, and the one you use to close gaps before they do.

## Insights

Insights aggregate across your whole program rather than a single framework, surfacing cross-cutting patterns: which controls carry the most weight, where effort is best spent, and how your posture has changed over time.

## Export audit-ready reports

When it's time for an audit, export your controls, evidence, and per-framework status in one package. The assessor gets a complete, current picture without you assembling screenshots, and you get a repeatable process for the next cycle.
