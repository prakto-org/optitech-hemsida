OptiTech is a compliance platform, not a database you attach an ORM to. You bring your tools and data into OptiTech in two ways:

- **Integrations** collect evidence automatically from the systems you already run — Microsoft 365, Entra ID, cloud providers, GitHub, and Swedish business systems. See [Connecting OptiTech to your stack](/docs/get-started/connect-neon).
- **The REST API** lets you automate OptiTech and move data in and out programmatically from any language.
