Sprinto wins teams on price and onboarding speed, and if that's how you chose it, apply the same test here: OptiTech's pricing is flat, published in SEK on the [pricing page](/pricing), and onboarding takes under a week. What changes is the ceiling. Sprinto gets you through a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit; it has no Swedish legal texts, no MSB or IMY filing, no Fortnox or BankID, and thin support for the supply chain duties NIS2 and DORA create.

## Before you export

- **Compare renewal math, not list price.** Sprinto is billed annually. Put your renewal quote next to [OptiTech's plans](/docs/introduction/plans) and include what you pay separately today (training tools, questionnaire hours) since those are included here.
- **If you're mid-audit, finish it in Sprinto.** Sprinto's async audit flow means an in-flight audit is cheapest to complete where it started. Migrate the week after the report lands.

<Steps>

## Export from Sprinto

Export controls with their check status, policies, personnel data, and your vendor list. Sprinto's exports are straightforward CSVs and documents; include evidence for the closed audit period as your archive.

## Import and upgrade the checks

Upload through **Settings**, then **Import**. Sprinto's checks map to OptiTech controls, and the assistant marks each one: covered by a continuous integration check here, or manual in both (imported as a task). Expect the automated share to grow, especially once the Swedish integrations come online.

## Activate NIS2 properly

If NIS2 is why you're moving, activate it now: requirements come from the Swedish legal text and MSBFS, and your imported ISO 27001 or SOC 2 controls pre-fill everything they genuinely satisfy. The gap analysis shows what's actually left.

## Connect the Swedish layer and cut over

Fortnox or Visma, BankID, Kivra. When your controls are green, cancel before the Sprinto renewal date.

</Steps>

## What changes when you switch

- **Same buying experience, higher ceiling**: transparent pricing and fast onboarding, plus the frameworks Sprinto doesn't reach
- **Authority reporting**: the MSB flow (24 hours, 72 hours, one month) and IMY's 72-hour flow, filed from the platform
- **Supply chain depth**: vendor questionnaires and the DORA ICT register when your customers start asking
- **EU data residency under EU ownership**, which Sprinto can't offer
