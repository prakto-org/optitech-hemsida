Cyberday and OptiTech overlap on the Nordic angle, so teams usually move for the automation depth: continuous technical evidence collection from your systems instead of task checklists, auto-remediation, Swedish incident flows to MSB and IMY, and the AI copilot.

## What you can import

- **Tasks and controls**: Cyberday's task-based model maps to OptiTech controls against your active frameworks
- **Policies**: exported documents imported with content intact, with version control and e-signing applied
- **Framework selections**: your active frameworks (NIS2, ISO 27001, DORA, GDPR) carry straight over
- **Vendor records**: your supplier list into the vendor register

<Steps>

## Export from Cyberday

Export your tasks, policies, and reports. Include assurance reports if you want them available as historical records.

## Upload to the import assistant

In the Console, open **Settings**, then **Import**, and upload the export. Tasks map to controls, and anything OptiTech can verify automatically through an integration switches from manual to continuous at cutover.

## Connect your integrations

This is the step that changes the day-to-day: connect Microsoft 365, your cloud, your code hosting, and the Swedish systems (Fortnox, Visma, BankID, Kivra). Controls that were manual check-offs become continuously verified.

## Verify and cut over

When your controls are green in OptiTech, cancel the old subscription.

</Steps>

## What you gain

- Continuous technical evidence collection instead of manual task completion
- Auto-remediation: fix failing controls via API or a ready-made ticket
- The MSB incident flow (24 hours, 72 hours, one month) and the IMY 72-hour flow
- The AI copilot for policy drafting and questionnaire answers
- Vendor risk management with automated questionnaires across your supply chain
