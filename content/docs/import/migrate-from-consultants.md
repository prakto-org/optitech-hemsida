Consultant engagements produce good material: a gap analysis, a policy package, a risk assessment. The problem is what happens next. The documents are accurate on delivery day and drift from reality every day after. This guide turns those deliverables into a program that maintains itself.

## What you can import

- **Gap analyses**: findings become tasks with owners and priorities
- **Policy packages**: delivered policies are imported with content intact, then put on review cycles with e-signing
- **Risk assessments**: risks and treatment plans into the risk register, linked to controls
- **Control descriptions**: mapped against OptiTech's framework requirements

<Steps>

## Gather the deliverables

Collect the final versions of what your consultants delivered. If the engagement produced a control matrix or statement of applicability, include it; it makes the mapping step faster.

## Upload and map

Upload the documents through **Settings**, then **Import**. The assistant extracts findings, controls, and risks, and proposes how they map to your active frameworks. You confirm before anything goes live.

## Assign owners

Consultant reports name functions; a running program needs people. Assign an owner to each imported control and task, so drift alerts reach someone who can act.

## Replace the annual review with continuous checks

Connect your [integrations](/docs/get-started/signing-up). What the consultant verified once, OptiTech now verifies continuously, and your documentation updates when regulations change instead of waiting for the next engagement.

</Steps>
