OptiTech's evidence engine is fully managed and connects to the systems you already run. Key capabilities include:

- **Automatic checks** — MFA coverage, offboarding within 24 hours, backup tests, encryption, and patch levels verified around the clock
- **Swedish integration suite** — Fortnox, Visma, BankID, and Kivra work out of the box, so checks run against your real employee register
- **Drift alerts** — when a control drifts, the owner is alerted the same morning, not at the next audit
- **Auto-remediation** — fix failing controls directly via API, or send a ready-made ticket to the right person
- **Tamper-evident log** — evidence lands in an append-only, hash-chained log that stands up to auditors and authorities
