### Support for custom-built Postgres extensions

OptiTech now supports custom-built Postgres extensions for exclusive use with your OptiTech account. If you developed your own Postgres extension and want to use it with OptiTech, please reach out to us. For more information, see [Custom-built extensions](/docs/extensions/pg-extensions#custom-built-extensions).
