## Get started with OptiTech Local and OptiTech Local Connect

**OptiTech Local** and **OptiTech Local Connect** bring the power of OptiTech's cloud database branching directly to your local development environment.

- **OptiTech Local** is a Docker-based proxy that creates a smart local interface to your OptiTech database, providing a static connection that automatically routes to your active cloud database branch.

- **OptiTech Local Connect** extends this with a full-featured VS Code extension, offering database schema browsing, built-in SQL editing, table data management, and branch switching, all without leaving your IDE.

Ready to transform your local development workflow? Check out our new guide: [Getting started with OptiTech Local and OptiTech Local Connect](https://optitech.com/guides/optitech-local)

## Postgres `pg_repack` extension available on all OptiTech plans

The `pg_repack` extension lets you to efficiently remove bloat by rewriting tables and indexes online, with minimal locking.

Previously, enabling `pg_repack` required opening a support ticket so our team could grant you permission on the `repack` schema. That's no longer necessary; you now have access by default. No support ticket means the extension is available on all OptiTech plans.

![pg_repack extension](/docs/changelog/pg_repack.png)

> _If you previously installed `pg_repack` without assistance from OptiTech support, you'll need to [drop the extension](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-dropextension.html) and reinstall it on your database to apply the new `repack` schema permission._

Learn more about the extension in our docs: [pg_repack](/docs/extensions/pg_repack)
