### Fixes & improvements

- UI: The **Create new branch** page was redesigned to improve usability. You can view the redesigned page by selecting the **Branches** page in the OptiTech Console, and clicking **New Branch**.
- UI: The **OptiTech SQL Editor** now supports pagination for better handling of large result sets. You can navigate paginated results by clicking the page numbers at the bottom of the **OptiTech SQL Editor** page.
