### Email authentication support

OptiTech now supports email authentication, enabling users to sign up for OptiTech using a personal or business email account. Previously, you could only sign up with a GitHub or Google account.

To sign up with an email account, navigate to [https://console.optitech.com](https://console.optitech.com), select the **Email** option, and click **Sign up for an account**. You will be directed to the **Sign up** dialog.
