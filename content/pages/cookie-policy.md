## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your settings and understand how it is used. We also use similar technologies, such as local storage, for the same purposes. This policy explains how OptiTech Sverige AB ("OptiTech", "we", "us"), registration number 559489-8917, Karlslundsvägen 8, 177 44 Stockholm, Sweden, uses these technologies on this website.

## How we ask for consent

The first time you visit the website, a cookie dialog asks for your choices. Strictly necessary cookies are always active. Optional cookies, such as analytics and marketing, are only set if you allow them, and you can allow each category separately under Customize.

Your choice is saved in the `ot_cookie_consent` cookie for 180 days. When it expires, the dialog asks again.

## Cookies we use

### Strictly necessary

Required for the website to work and for remembering your choices. These cannot be turned off.

| Name                | Type          | Purpose                                        | Duration  |
| ------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `ot_cookie_consent` | Cookie        | Stores your cookie consent choices             | 180 days  |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`       | Cookie        | Remembers your language choice (English or Swedish) | 12 months |
| `ot-theme`          | Local storage | Remembers your light or dark theme choice in the docs | Until cleared |

Some interactive features, such as checklists in the documentation, also save their state in your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device.

### Analytics

Help us understand how the website is used so we can improve it, such as which pages are visited, how visitors navigate, and which content performs well. Analytics cookies are only set with your consent.

### Marketing

Used to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and show more relevant content, for example by recognizing that you clicked a link in one of our campaigns. Marketing cookies are only set with your consent.

## Managing your choices

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time:

- Delete the `ot_cookie_consent` cookie in your browser settings. The cookie dialog appears again on your next visit, where you can make new choices per category
- Block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or clear cookies when you close the browser. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies can affect how the website works

Withdrawing consent doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before the withdrawal.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our use of cookies changes, for example when we add or remove a tool. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top. Material changes trigger a new consent request.
