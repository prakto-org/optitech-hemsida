## How to get audit-ready faster

Getting audit-ready faster is a whole-organization job. For compliance, your real-world speed depends primarily on three things:

1. **Scoping Approach:** A precise picture of which laws apply, or guesswork and over-documentation?
2. **Evidence-System Proximity:** How close is your evidence to the systems that produce it?
3. **Time Spent per Requirement:** How much time does your team spend answering each requirement?

### Establishing scope

Before you can prove anything, you need to know what applies. This process of mapping laws, categories, and requirements to your business traditionally takes several back and forth rounds between your team and a consultant.

**A traditional scoping engagement means many roundtrips:**
![Many round-trips between your team and a consultant in a traditional scoping engagement](/faster/postgres-tcp-roundtrips.jpg)

When you combine many roundtrips with consultant scheduling you get a compounding effect on calendar time. How often you must re-scope depends on how you run compliance:

#### Continuous scoping

Organizations on a compliance platform follow a pattern of establishing scope once and keeping it continuously updated as regulations and the business change. This makes re-scoping a non-factor in the day-to-day experience of your program.

#### Project-based scoping

Consultant engagements execute independently as isolated projects, so it's not feasible to keep scope current. Every new framework, customer requirement, or regulation change triggers a new engagement.

<Admonition type="tip" title="Tips for faster scoping">
1. Answer the 20-question gap analysis in one sitting with someone who knows your systems
2. Map several frameworks in the same pass - so you only pay the scoping tax once.
3. Let cross-mapping do the work - to reduce the number of requirements you answer by hand.
   ![Fewer round-trips with a guided automated flow](/faster/postgres-http-roundtrips.jpg)
   OptiTech's gap analysis and cross-mapping engine have been optimized to reduce the number of manual steps to the absolute minimum. To see what a guided flow looks like in practice, see: [MSB incident reporting](/services/incident-reporting)
</Admonition>

### Minimizing time spent per requirement

As your business (and framework count) grows, scoping is a one-time cost and time per requirement becomes the most important factor to optimize. Here are some pointers:

1. **Use cross-mapping and automation** – Let one control satisfy overlapping requirements across frameworks, and enable OptiTech's continuous checks so evidence is collected on demand.
2. **Profile slow controls** – Identify which controls consume the most manual hours by starting with the compliance dashboard in OptiTech.
   ![OptiTech query performance tab](/docs/introduction/query_performance.png)
   _The dashboard shows status, owner, and time since last verification for each control._
   You can dig deeper in each control's evidence history
3. **Automate high-frequency evidence** – Connect the systems that generate recurring proof (identity, endpoints, cloud, HR) so checks like MFA coverage and offboarding run without human effort, dramatically reducing recurring workload.
4. **Retire stale documents** – Reclaim wasted effort by eliminating duplicate and outdated policies. Schedule the annual review cycle with reminders and e-signing so nothing drifts, and let version history keep the record clean over time.
5. **Answer questionnaires from live data** – Ensure incoming security questionnaires are drafted from your verified controls instead of from scratch. OptiTech's AI copilot writes the answers and cites the evidence, so aim to keep your control base current. You can monitor coverage on the dashboard to see how much of a questionnaire can be answered automatically and adjust your integrations if needed.

### Evidence automation

When a control in OptiTech is connected to the system that produces its proof, evidence is collected and verified automatically.
This is useful for:

- **Resource Management** - Keeping evidence current is automatic.
- **Cost-Efficiency** - Never pay for consultant hours spent chasing screenshots.

But evidence automation is only useful if failures surface quickly when they happen. That's why [incident readiness](#incident-readiness) matters so much.

### Incident readiness

An incident in OptiTech begins when a monitored control fails or your team reports an event.
OptiTech starts the guided flow, prefills the authority forms, and counts down every statutory deadline.
The flow stays active until the final report is filed.

#### Applications of evidence automation

The answer depends on the specifics of your business.

- **Supplier Assessments** - Customer questionnaires, procurement requirements, due diligence requests.
