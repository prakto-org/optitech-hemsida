## Quick answer

OptiTech loads the CRI Profile tier-aware: the impact assessment determines your tier, the applicable diagnostic statements load as requirements, and your existing controls cross-map onto them, with the [NIST CSF](/faqs/nist-csf-compliance-optitech) function structure as the shared skeleton. Evidence collects continuously, and the Profile's maintained mappings mean the same control state renders as a Profile assessment, a [23 NYCRR 500 posture](/faqs/23-nycrr-500-compliance-optitech), or a [DORA view](/faqs/dora-compliance-optitech), whichever the examiner or counterparty speaks.

## Tiering as configuration, not negotiation

The impact questionnaire (services, interconnectedness, scale) computes your tier and documents the reasoning, so the scoping conversation with an examiner starts from a defensible record. Tier changes (growth, new critical services) arrive through the same [scope-change mechanics](/faqs/databases-automatically-scale-serverless-environments) as everything else: a reviewed delta of newly applicable diagnostic statements rather than a program rebuild.

## One assessment, many audiences

The Profile's harmonization pays off operationally when each audience gets its view from the same data:

- **Examiners** see the Profile assessment with per-statement status and [timestamped evidence](/faqs/databases-reproduce-bugs-production-data), sampled through [scoped read-only access](/faqs/find-database-url-neon).
- **Counterparties** running due diligence get [questionnaire answers from live control state](/faqs/enable-pgvector-extension) or a [Trust Center view](/faqs/find-database-connection-string-url).
- **The board** gets the function-level trend reporting financial regulators expect of governance, generated from the same posture; see [board reporting](/faqs/find-database-connection-string).

## The financial-sector control depth

The Profile's heavier expectations run on the platform's standard machinery at financial-grade settings: access and privileged-account checks continuous against your identity provider, [incident response with regulatory clocks](/faqs/debug-production-database-issues-safely) per applicable regime, third-party risk through the [supplier register with DORA-grade contract tracking](/faqs/dora-compliance-optitech), and resilience testing on a [managed calendar](/faqs/iso-22301-compliance-optitech). For technology vendors assessed in Profile terms by financial customers, the same setup answers the diligence without a bespoke project per counterparty.
