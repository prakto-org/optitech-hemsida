## Quick answer

Cyber Essentials' five themes are almost entirely machine-verifiable, which makes it a natural fit for OptiTech's [integration checks](/faqs/best-postgres-services-connection-pooling): firewall and configuration state from your cloud, access control and MFA from your identity provider, malware protection and patch status from your MDM and endpoint tools. The self-assessment answers come from live control status, and the annual renewal is a [calendar event](/faqs/pci-dss-compliance-optitech), not a project.

## The checks behind the five themes

- **Access control**: MFA coverage, unique accounts, admin separation, and [offboarding within 24 hours](/faqs/best-postgres-services-connection-pooling) verified against Entra ID or Google Workspace.
- **Secure configuration**: cloud and device baseline checks: default credentials, exposed services, device lock policies from [Intune or Jamf](/faqs/connect-application-using-connection-string).
- **Update management**: the 14-day high-severity patch requirement runs as a per-device check with an actual clock: devices out of window become [findings routed to IT](/faqs/databases-avoid-connection-limits-serverless-applications), before the assessment asks.
- **Malware protection**: endpoint protection presence and status from CrowdStrike or Defender.
- **Firewalls**: boundary configuration checks from your cloud integrations.

Unsupported software, the silent Cyber Essentials killer, surfaces through inventory checks: operating systems and applications past end-of-support get flagged as they age out, not when the questionnaire asks.

## Scope honesty, including BYOD

The assessment scope includes devices that touch organizational data. The [asset inventory](/faqs/databases-automatically-scale-serverless-environments) gives you a defensible scope statement: managed devices enumerate from the MDM, cloud services from the integrations, and the BYOD question gets answered by policy plus conditional-access checks rather than optimism.

## Plus, and beyond

For Cyber Essentials Plus, the independent tester verifies what your dashboard already shows, so surprises are rare and remediation pre-empted. And since the five themes are a strict subset of [ISO 27001](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech) and [NIS 2](/faqs/nis-2-compliance-optitech) territory, the certificate is a cheap, UK-recognized byproduct of the program you run anyway, worth holding if UK deals are in your pipeline.
