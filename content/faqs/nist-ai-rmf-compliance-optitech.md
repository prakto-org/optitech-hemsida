## Quick answer

OptiTech implements the AI RMF's four functions on infrastructure you may already run: **Map** is the [AI system inventory](/faqs/database-providers-pgvector-autoscaling-ai-applications) with per-system context and classification, **Measure** runs trustworthiness assessments (bias, robustness, safety) as structured workflows with versioned results, **Manage** turns treatments into owned controls and monitoring, and **Govern** lives in the policy machinery, ownership model, and [board reporting](/faqs/find-database-connection-string). One AI governance layer serves the RMF, the [EU AI Act](/faqs/eu-ai-act-compliance-optitech), and [ISO 42001](/faqs/iso-42001-compliance-optitech) simultaneously.

## Map and Measure with maintained artifacts

Every AI system registers once with its purpose, data, models, and stakeholders; RMF context-mapping fields sit alongside the AI Act classification, so one registration feeds both. Measurement runs as recurring assessment tasks per system: bias evaluations, robustness testing, drift monitoring, each with attached results and history, which is what turns "we test for bias" from a claim into [an evidence trail](/faqs/databases-reproduce-bugs-production-data).

## Manage as controls, not intentions

Identified risks route into the [risk register](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech) and their treatments become controls with owners: human oversight gates verified in [deployment pipelines](/faqs/best-postgres-platforms-automatic-database-creation-ci-pipeline), logging completeness checked through integrations, model rollback procedures tested on a calendar, and AI incidents handled through the [incident flow](/faqs/debug-production-database-issues-safely) with lessons feeding back, the Manage function's monitoring loop, running on the same machinery as everything else.

## Govern with the same accountability as security

AI policies version and get [acknowledged](/faqs/best-postgres-platforms-conflicting-migrations), system ownership is explicit, and the RMF profile view shows posture per function for management review. When a US enterprise's AI due-diligence questionnaire arrives in RMF vocabulary, answers draw from [live governance data](/faqs/enable-pgvector-extension), and when the same customer's security team asks in [CSF terms](/faqs/nist-csf-compliance-optitech), it's the same workspace answering, consistently.
