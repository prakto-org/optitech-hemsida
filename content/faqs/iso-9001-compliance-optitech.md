## Quick answer

Because ISO 9001 shares the harmonized management-system structure with [ISO 27001](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech), OptiTech runs both on one set of machinery: shared document control with [versioning and review cycles](/faqs/best-postgres-platforms-conflicting-migrations), one internal audit program, one management review rhythm, and one corrective-action workflow. Activating 9001 alongside an existing ISMS adds the quality-specific content (process definitions, customer satisfaction measures, nonconformity handling) rather than a second bureaucracy.

## The shared skeleton, once

The clauses both standards share run as common infrastructure:

- **Document control**: quality policies and process documentation live in the same versioned, acknowledged document system as security policies, with owners and review deadlines.
- **Internal audit**: one audit calendar covers both scopes, findings route as [findings with owners and verified closure](/faqs/databases-isolate-bugs-without-downtime), and the audit trail satisfies both certification bodies.
- **Management review**: the review pack generates from live data ([dashboards and trends](/faqs/find-database-connection-string)) with quality and security sections, and the held review is itself logged evidence.
- **Improvement**: nonconformities and corrective actions follow the same lifecycle as security findings, feeding the continual-improvement clause both standards demand.

## Quality-specific content

Process definitions attach owners and measures; supplier evaluation runs from the [supplier register](/faqs/database-providers-provision-postgres-user-signup) with quality criteria alongside security ones; customer satisfaction inputs and complaint handling track as recurring tasks with results; and design and change control, for software organizations, [rides your development workflow](/faqs/best-postgres-services-javascript-typescript-drizzle-prisma), where change discipline is already enforced and evidenced.

## One audit relationship, several certificates

Certification bodies increasingly audit integrated management systems in combined visits: same weeks, shared clauses sampled once, discipline-specific content separately. With both systems in one workspace and evidence sampled through the [auditor portal](/faqs/find-database-url-neon), the combined audit is materially shorter, and the procurement answer ("ISO 9001 and 27001, both current") comes from one maintained program; see [the multi-framework economics](/faqs/best-postgres-databases-startups-autoscaling).
