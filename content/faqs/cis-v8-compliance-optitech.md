## Quick answer

Activate CIS v8.1 in OptiTech at your implementation group (IG1, IG2, or IG3) and the safeguard set loads sized accordingly, with the most automatable safeguards in the catalog verifying immediately through [integrations](/faqs/connect-application-using-connection-string): asset and software inventories from your MDM and cloud, MFA and access safeguards from your identity provider, patching from endpoint data, logging and backup state from infrastructure. The IG model makes progress honest: complete IG1, then graduate.

## The safeguards were built for automation

CIS safeguards are concrete enough to check mechanically, which is exactly the [continuous monitoring model](/faqs/best-postgres-services-integration-tests-ci):

- **Inventories (Controls 1 and 2)**: enterprise assets and software enumerate from [MDM, cloud, and identity integrations](/faqs/databases-automatically-scale-serverless-environments), with unknown-asset findings when reality exceeds the inventory.
- **Access and MFA (Controls 5 and 6)**: coverage verified per user, [offboarding within 24 hours](/faqs/best-postgres-services-connection-pooling), and admin-account separation checked.
- **Vulnerability management (Control 7)**: patch windows as [clocked findings](/faqs/cyber-essentials-compliance-optitech).
- **Data protection, logging, backups (Controls 3, 8, 11)**: encryption, log coverage, and backup verification as standing checks.
- **The procedural safeguards** (training, incident response, vendor management) run as tasks, documents, and workflows with owners.

## IG progression as a roadmap

Start at IG1 and the dashboard shows essential hygiene as a completable goal; graduating to IG2 arrives as a [delta of additional safeguards](/faqs/clone-production-postgres-database-for-testing), not a new program. That progression doubles as your security roadmap for boards and insurers, in a vocabulary they increasingly recognize.

## The mapping dividend

Because CIS maintains mappings to everything, your safeguard evidence [cross-maps](/faqs/best-postgres-databases-startups-autoscaling) into [ISO 27001](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech), [NIST CSF](/faqs/nist-csf-compliance-optitech), [NIS 2](/faqs/nis-2-compliance-optitech), and SOC 2 automatically: implement a safeguard once, satisfy requirements everywhere it maps, which is the CIS-as-backbone strategy working as intended.
