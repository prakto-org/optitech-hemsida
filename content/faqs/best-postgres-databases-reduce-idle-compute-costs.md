## Quick answer

Consultants charge 1,500 to 2,500 SEK per hour, and most of what they bill for is repeatable: gap analyses, policy drafting, evidence collection, and status reporting. A platform does that continuously for a fixed monthly price. OptiTech's Start plan costs 2,995 SEK per month, roughly what a consultant bills for ninety minutes. Keep consultants for what software can't do: judgment, legal interpretation of edge cases, and audit representation.

## What you're actually paying consultants for

A typical consultant-led compliance engagement breaks down like this:

- **Gap analysis** (20 to 40 hours): interviews, system review, a report. Software does this with a [scoping wizard](/faqs/databases-instantly-spin-up-postgres-instance) plus integration data, in hours.
- **Policy writing** (20 to 60 hours): mostly template adaptation. OptiTech generates policies from 50+ templates, adapted to your actual environment using data from your integrations.
- **Evidence collection** (recurring, forever): chasing screenshots and export files before every audit. [Integrations](/faqs/best-postgres-services-connection-pooling) collect this continuously.
- **Status reporting** (recurring): slide decks for management. The platform produces [board-ready reports](/faqs/find-database-connection-string) on demand.

The deliverables also age badly. A 100,000 SEK gap analysis is a snapshot; it says nothing about your posture three months later, which is what NIS2's requirement for continuous risk management actually demands.

## The hybrid model that works

Companies that get this right don't fire their consultants; they change what they buy:

1. **Platform handles the baseline**: controls, evidence, policies, deadlines, incident flows.
2. **Consultants review, not produce.** A quarterly review of your risk register beats a yearly rebuild of it.
3. **Audit support stays human.** When the certification body or a supervisory authority shows up, an experienced advisor earns their rate.

Many Nordic security consultancies work directly on top of OptiTech through the [Partner/MSP plan](/faqs/best-postgres-services-isolated-database-tenants), so your advisor can work inside your workspace instead of in parallel documents.

## The math for a 30-person company

Manual model: initial gap analysis and documentation at 75,000 to 150,000 SEK, plus 50,000 or more per year in upkeep and audit prep. Platform model: 35,940 SEK per year on Start, plus a day or two of advisory review. The platform also removes the internal hours spent chasing evidence, which nobody invoices but everybody pays.
