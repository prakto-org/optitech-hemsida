## Quick answer

NIS 2 is OptiTech's home framework: the requirement catalog is built from the Swedish Cybersecurity Act and MSB's regulations (not a generic translation of the directive), scoping determines your entity classification, the measures run as [continuously verified controls](/faqs/best-postgres-services-integration-tests-ci), and the incident flow implements the actual Swedish reporting sequence: 24-hour early warning, 72-hour notification, one-month final report, with pre-filled authority forms and deadline countdowns.

## Scoping and the management layer

The [onboarding wizard](/faqs/databases-instantly-spin-up-postgres-instance) determines whether you're in scope, your likely essential-or-important classification, and which measures apply proportionately. The management duties get their own machinery: board-facing [reporting](/faqs/find-database-connection-string) that demonstrates active oversight, training modules for management (a NIS 2 requirement with personal liability behind it), and documented approval of the risk measures, the paper trail that protects your board members personally.

## The measures on automation

The directive's measure list maps directly onto platform mechanics: risk analysis in the [risk register](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech), incident handling below, continuity and backups verified through [cloud integrations](/faqs/connect-application-using-connection-string), supply chain security in the [supplier register with questionnaires](/faqs/database-providers-provision-postgres-user-signup), hygiene and MFA checked continuously against your identity provider, and training tracked per employee. Evidence accumulates in the [append-only log](/faqs/databases-reproduce-bugs-production-data), which is what a supervisory authority means when it asks you to demonstrate compliance over time.

## The incident flow, rehearsed and ready

When an incident opens, the [record](/faqs/debug-production-database-issues-safely) runs the NIS 2 track: significance assessment against the reporting criteria, countdown timers per deadline, MSB report forms pre-filled from the timeline, jour contact lists, and communication templates. The one-month final report assembles from the record instead of from memory. Run the built-in tabletop exercise before the real thing; the 24-hour clock is unforgiving to first-timers.

## The supply chain, both directions

In-scope entities send [structured questionnaires](/faqs/database-providers-provision-postgres-user-signup) to suppliers; suppliers answer once and reuse. If NIS 2 reached you through a customer's contract rather than the annexes, the same mechanics answer their demands, and your posture becomes [shareable proof](/faqs/best-managed-postgres-databases-multi-tenant-saas) for the next customer who asks. Financial-sector relationships layer [DORA](/faqs/dora-compliance-optitech) on the same control set.
