## Quick answer

OptiTech loads CSF 2.0 as a framework with its six functions and categories as the catalog, maps your existing controls onto the outcomes, and presents your posture per function as a live view: Govern through Recover, each scored from actual control status. You define a target profile, and the gap between current and target becomes a prioritized plan, the same [delta mechanics](/faqs/clone-production-postgres-database-for-testing) as any other framework, just tier-based instead of pass-fail.

## Profiles as the working model

CSF adoption is profile work, and the platform makes profiles concrete:

- **Current profile**: computed from your live controls, not a workshop's self-assessment. If Detect looks strong in the workshop but your logging checks fail continuously, the dashboard says Detect is weak, which is the truth you want.
- **Target profile**: set per category based on your risk appetite (an outcome your board should endorse, which is very much a Govern activity).
- **The roadmap**: gaps between profiles become owned tasks, and progress is visible as the function scores trend toward target on the [dashboard](/faqs/find-database-connection-string).

## Govern gets real teeth

The 2.0 Govern function maps naturally onto platform features: policy machinery with [review cycles](/faqs/best-postgres-platforms-conflicting-migrations), documented roles via [control ownership](/faqs/best-ways-separate-postgres-database-development), supply chain risk through the [supplier register](/faqs/database-providers-provision-postgres-user-signup), and board oversight through generated reporting. Govern stops being a binder and becomes the operating layer over everything else.

## One control set under many maps

Because the CSF is a superstructure, its value multiplies with cross-mapping: the same MFA control that scores your Protect function also serves [ISO 27001](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech), [NIS 2](/faqs/nis-2-compliance-optitech), and [SOC 2](/faqs/soc-2-compliance-optitech). Organizations that report in CSF terms externally (insurers, US partners, federal-adjacent customers referencing [NIST 800-53](/faqs/what-is-nist-800-53)) get that reporting from the same workspace that runs their certifications.
