## Why opaque pricing hurts smaller companies

The traditional GRC sales model was built for enterprises: a discovery call, a custom quote, a negotiation, an annual contract. For a 30-person company that just got a NIS2 questionnaire from its biggest customer, that process is friction at exactly the wrong moment. You don't know if the tool costs 50,000 or 250,000 SEK per year until you've sat through a demo.

Transparent pricing changes the decision:

- You can budget before you evaluate.
- You can compare plans against consultant quotes on equal terms.
- You can start on a small plan and upgrade when a new framework or customer requirement lands.
