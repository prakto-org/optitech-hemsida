## Quick answer

OptiTech supports HITRUST at all three levels: activate e1, i1, or r2 and the corresponding requirement catalog loads, cross-mapped against your existing controls, so [HIPAA](/faqs/hipaa-compliance-optitech), [ISO 27001](/faqs/iso-27001-compliance-optitech), and [SOC 2](/faqs/soc-2-compliance-optitech) work you've already done counts immediately. Evidence collects continuously with the timestamps and history HITRUST assessors sample, and the readiness view shows per-requirement maturity before you engage the external assessor.

## Level-aware from the start

You pick the target level and the platform scopes accordingly: e1's essentials list, i1's broader implemented set, or r2's risk-tailored requirements driven by your scoping factors (data volumes, system exposure, regulatory context). Upgrading levels later is a [delta, not a restart](/faqs/best-postgres-databases-startups-autoscaling): the i1 evidence base carries into r2, with the gap list showing only what r2 adds for your risk profile.

## Maturity, not just pass-fail

HITRUST scores policy, process, and implementation maturity per control. OptiTech tracks all three dimensions: policies live in the [versioned document machinery](/faqs/best-postgres-platforms-conflicting-migrations), process evidence accumulates from workflows (reviews completed, incidents handled, training done), and implementation verifies through [integration checks](/faqs/best-postgres-services-connection-pooling) against your identity provider, cloud, and MDM. The readiness dashboard shows where maturity is thin before the assessor bills you for the same discovery.

## Working with your external assessor

HITRUST requires an approved external assessor, and the engagement runs better when evidence is self-serve: grant [scoped, read-only access](/faqs/find-database-url-neon) to the in-scope controls and their history. PHI-bearing system scoping from your [HIPAA setup](/faqs/hipaa-compliance-optitech) carries over directly, since HITRUST's boundary questions are the same ones.

## Keeping certification current

e1 and i1 renew annually, r2 has an interim assessment: all of it lands in the recurring calendar with owners and deadlines, and because evidence never stopped collecting, renewal is [review work, not archaeology](/faqs/best-postgres-services-eliminate-shared-staging-database).
