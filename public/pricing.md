## Plan details

### Start

Start costs 2,995 kr/month and is designed for companies with 5-30 employees implementing their first framework. It includes one framework, 10 integrations, automated gap analysis, Swedish policy templates, incident reporting, a risk register, BankID login, and EU data residency.

### Professional

Professional costs 7,995 kr/month and supports companies with 30-150 employees managing several frameworks. It includes three frameworks, all integrations, vendor risk management, Trust Center, security awareness training, AI copilot, and auto-remediation.

### Enterprise

Enterprise starts at 19,995 kr/month for companies with 150+ employees and regulated financial institutions. It includes unlimited frameworks, the DORA ICT contract register, auditor portal, SSO and SCIM, API and CLI access, custom onboarding, and a dedicated customer success manager.
